## Project Overview

This project analyzes the sales and stock performance of Wanjiku's vegetable stall at Gikomba Market. The aim is to understand sales trends, compare weekday and weekend performance, identify strong and weak sales days, evaluate stock levels and determine which products need reordering. Using core Python, the analysis provides business insights to help Wanjiku make better purchasing decisions, manage inventory efficiently and improve overall business performance.

## How to Run

Open the notebook in Google Colab: [Open stall_analysis.ipynb in Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/github/nelly-mwangi/plp-da-a1-gikomba-stall-analysis/blob/main/stall_analysis.ipynb)

## Top Three Findings

1. **Total Sales:** The stall generated total sales of **KES** 46,**450**, with average daily sales of **KES** 3,**317**.86.

2. **Weekend Performance:** Average weekend sales were **KES** 4,**900** compared to **KES** 2,**685** on weekdays. Weekend sales were approximately 82.50% higher.

3. **Weekly Growth:** Sales increased from **KES** 22,**050** in Week 1 to **KES** 24,**400** in Week 2, representing a growth of 10.66%.

## Conclusion

The analysis shows that Wanjiku can improve her stall's performance by preparing for higher weekend demand, monitoring weekly sales growth and prioritizing the restocking of products such as Tomatoes and Sukuma. These recommendations are intended to support better inventory management and increased business profitability.
